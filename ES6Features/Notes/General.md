# Toyota Early Models: Detailed Specifications

## 1936 Toyoda Model AA
* **Body Style:** 4-door sedan
* **Transmission:** 3-speed manual
* **Chassis:** Ladder frame
* **Suspension:** Rigid axles with leaf springs (front and rear)
* **Top Speed:** Approximately 100 km/h (62 mph)

## 1947 Toyota Model SA
* **Body Style:** 2-door sedan
* **Transmission:** 3-speed manual
* **Chassis:** Backbone chassis
* **Suspension:** Independent front suspension (four-wheel independent setup)
* **Significance:** Toyota's first passenger car utilizing independent suspension components.

## 1951 Toyota Land Cruiser (BJ Series)
* **Body Style:** Open 2-door 4×4
* **Transmission:** 4-speed manual
* **Drive Configuration:** Part-time four-wheel drive
* **Torque Output:** 211 N⋅m (156 lb⋅ft) @ 1,600 rpm
* **Significance:** Built to handle extreme off-road terrain, establishing the brand's reputation for rugged durability.

## 1955 Toyota Crown (RS Series)
* **Body Style:** 4-door sedan (featuring rear suicide doors)
* **Transmission:** 3-speed manual (column shift)
* **Suspension:** Double wishbone front suspension with coil springs
* **Weight:** 1,210 kg (2,668 lbs)

## 1966 Toyota Corolla (KE10)
* **Body Style:** 2-door sedan / 4-door sedan / station wagon
* **Transmission:** 4-speed manual (floor shift) or 2-speed automatic
* **Suspension:** MacPherson strut front suspension (revolutionary for compact cars at the time)
* **Weight:** 690 kg (1,521 lbs)

## 1967 Toyota 2000GT (MF10)
* **Body Style:** 2-door fastback coupe
* **Transmission:** 5-speed manual or 3-speed automatic
* **Torque Output:** 175 N⋅m (129 lb⋅ft) @ 5,000 rpm
* **Brakes:** Four-wheel Dunlop disc brakes (a first for a Japanese production car)
* **Top Speed:** 220 km/h (137 mph)
