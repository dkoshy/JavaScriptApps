Our History

For many , years Bethany has been backing delicious pies at her home.

In 2013 , we opened our first shop in Kerala ,Trivandrum in a cozy little street near central market. People from all over the world fell in love with this soft cakes, heavenly cheese cakes, spicy apple pies ...etcThe store became a success in no time.

In 2015, no less than 100 pice where sold every day and bethany's pie shop moved into a new building. After receiving many request from our customer to making it possible to order and select best ingredients for the recipe from the comfort of your home and from anywhere in the world. As a result we are available online and you can try out our delicious pies.
